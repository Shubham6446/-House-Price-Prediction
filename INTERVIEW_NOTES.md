# Interview notes

- Problem: estimate a house's price from property features.
- Inputs/features: Area, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, Age.
- Target: Price.
- Algorithm: Linear Regression (supervised regression).
- Train/test split: 80% / 20%.
- Metrics: MAE (average absolute prediction error) and R² (variance explained relative to the mean baseline).
- Always quote the MAE and R² printed by your own run. They depend on the dataset and split.
